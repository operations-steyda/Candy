Colored rows are shipped to Amazon (ignored). Every uncolored row is deducted from Production Qty — both already-produced (packing date) and for-production next week (no packing date; calendar week in column A).
◧ Staged units netted out of Production Qty — by SKU
Warehouse productAmazon SKUStaged cartonsStaged pouches (netted)
Each deducted warehouse product is mapped to its Amazon SKU and subtracted from that SKU's gross Production Qty (floored at 0). Every not-shipped (uncolored) carton is deducted — already produced and for-production alike. Cover, stockout and status are untouched.
◧ Shipment log
WeekShip #ContentsCartonsPouchesPackedShippedState
Shipped rows are colored and have left for Amazon FBA (never deducted). Staged rows are uncolored with a packing date — already produced and waiting at the warehouse. Planned rows are uncolored with no packing date — for production next week (calendar week in column A). Both Staged and Planned are netted out of Production Qty; only Shipped is ignored.
